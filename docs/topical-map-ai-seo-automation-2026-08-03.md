## Pillar

### `/ai-seo-automation`

- Primary intent: understand and evaluate AI SEO automation.
- Funnel role: commercial investigation with a complete educational framework.
- Primary answer: automate bounded, repeatable work under evidence, policy, permission, approval, validation, and measurement controls.
- Original evidence: the internal 13-candidate multi-URL test and its corrected promotion gate.
- Product boundary: public homepage audit available; governed lifecycle and simulation implemented; live GitHub and WordPress adapters disabled.
- Conversion: free Growth Audit and simulated workflow demo.

The pillar links in body context to every supporting guide. It does not claim that live external execution is available.

## Postponed or excluded

- Persona variants such as “AI SEO automation for startups/agencies/ecommerce” until Search Console or SERP evidence shows distinct intent.
- Tool-vs-tool automation comparison pages without first-hand testing.
- Programmatic “automate [task]” pages.
- A standalone “SEO action queue” page until query demand supports it; the approval-workflow guide covers the operational job.
- A technical SEO automation pillar until the crawler and technical-action pipeline can provide publishable first-hand evidence.
- Claims of autonomous GitHub, WordPress, CMS, schema, or content publishing while those live adapters remain disabled.

## Measurement

Measure the cluster as a page group after deployment:

- indexation and canonical state for new URLs
- internal-link crawlability

- irrelevant or machine-shaped queries that should not affect content decisions

Apply a 14-day cooldown before another material rewrite of the pillar or repaired support pages. Do not claim ranking impact until a complete post-change Search Console window is available.
