## Strategic recommendation

1. Free Growth Audit: available now at $0.

## Content gaps in current ranking pages

The visible search results for pricing queries are dominated by listicles, affiliate-style comparison pages, and individual vendor pricing pages. Common gaps:

- monthly prices are mixed with annual-equivalent prices without a clear distinction;
- usage units such as prompts, pages, articles, seats, crawl credits, and domains are not compared in one place;
- AI visibility is often treated as a feature checkbox rather than a metered workflow;
- dashboard, content-generation, automation, AI-agent, and agency categories are blended together;
- limitations, add-ons, overages, tax, and sales-led pricing are underexplained;
- few pages explain the cost of the human work required after a report or draft is produced;
- competitor claims are often copied without an access date or direct official source;
- few pages give a credible answer for SaaS, e-commerce, agencies, startups, and solo founders separately;
- almost no comparison explains what an AI growth operator is buying beyond another dashboard;
- product screenshots are frequently generic or fabricated rather than real, attributable product evidence.

## Original points of view to own

1. Compare the unit of value, not just the monthly number.
2. Explain tool versus AI growth operator: data and drafts versus a governed action loop.
3. Compare software cost with agency capacity without pretending they are interchangeable.
4. Put SEO and GEO in one workflow, but distinguish monitoring from execution.
5. Use the free audit as an evidence-first entry point, not a rankings promise.
6. Show early-stage pricing honestly: target pricing is not active subscription pricing.
7. Tell readers who should not choose SERP Strategists yet.
8. Make human approval and rollback a buying criterion for execution software.
