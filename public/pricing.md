# Pricing - SERP Strategists

Last verified: July 27, 2026

## Position

SERP Strategists is building an AI Growth Operator for organic search. It combines SEO auditing, opportunity prioritization, governed execution, content workflows, GEO and AI-visibility monitoring, and outcome measurement.

## Availability

- The free Growth Audit is available now.
- Growth and Scale are early-access target prices for approved accounts.
- Joining early access does not start a subscription or charge a card.
- Final scope, activation date, included limits, and billing start are confirmed before activation.
- No paid-plan free trial, annual discount, or automatic overage pricing is currently published.

## SERP Strategists plans

### Audit

- Price: $0
- Availability: available now
- Scope: one submitted website
- Includes: technical SEO and GEO readiness review; impact-ranked opportunity queue
- Does not include: automated fixes or a paid subscription

### Growth

- Target price: $49/month
- Availability: early-access target
- Scope: one connected site
- Target capacity: approval-gated technical and content actions; up to four content actions per month; monitoring, action logs, outcome windows, and email summaries

### Scale

- Target price: $99/month
- Availability: early-access target
- Scope: up to three connected sites
- Target capacity: everything in Growth; AI citation and prompt monitoring; up to twelve content actions per month; competitor source-gap monitoring; priority onboarding support
